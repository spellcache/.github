# Code of Conduct

The spellcache project adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct, for every repository, issue, discussion and pull
request of the organisation.

In short: be respectful and welcoming, assume good faith, give and receive
constructive criticism gracefully, and focus on what is best for the
community. Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and publishing
others' private information are not tolerated.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the
maintainers at **contact@spellcache.com**. All
reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers apply the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1
(correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban), depending on the impact of
the behaviour.
